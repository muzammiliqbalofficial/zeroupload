100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture

Remove PDF Pages runs as JavaScript and WebAssembly already loaded into this page. There is no upload step: the file you add is read directly into the browser's memory, processed there, and turned into a download link, without a request ever leaving your device to send the document anywhere.

Worth Knowing
  • Page numbers refer to the original document. Click thumbnails to toggle them, or type a list like 2, 5, 8 or a range like 3-7 directly into the field.
  • Removing pages only rebuilds the page tree — the content of every page you keep is byte-for-byte the same as before.
  • If the PDF needs a password just to open, unlock it first with the Unlock PDF tool, then remove pages from that copy.
Frequently Asked Questions
Are deleted pages recoverable?Once you download the new file, deleted pages are permanently stripped from the new output. Keep your original if you might need those pages later.
Does removing pages shrink file size?Yes, roughly in proportion to what those pages contained — a few text pages barely change the total, while removing image-heavy pages can cut it noticeably.
Can I remove a range of pages at once?Yes. Select several thumbnails, or enter a range such as 10-15 alongside individual page numbers.
