100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture

Organize PDF Pages runs as JavaScript and WebAssembly already loaded into this page. There is no upload step: the file you add is read directly into the browser's memory, processed there, and turned into a download link, without a request ever leaving your device to send the document anywhere.

Worth Knowing
  • Every change — reordering, rotating, deleting — happens on the visible grid before you save, so you can review the final page order before committing to a download.
  • Made a mistake? Undo (or Ctrl+Z) steps back through your last 30 changes one at a time. "Reset Order" is different — it discards everything and starts over from the original document.
  • Deleting a page here removes it from the saved output only; nothing is destroyed until you click Save & Download.
Frequently Asked Questions
How fast is thumbnail rendering?Thumbnails are rendered asynchronously in your browser in seconds, even for documents with hundreds of pages.
Are original page qualities preserved?Yes. Reordering, rotating, and deleting pages doesn't touch the content of the pages you keep — output resolution is identical to the source.
Can I undo a rotation or deletion after saving?Undo only works before you download. Once you save, treat it as final — keep your original file if you might need it again.
