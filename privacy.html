Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: ZeroUpload is built from the ground up so that your files never leave your device. Every document transformation occurs 100% locally within your browser using WebAssembly. There are no backend storage servers, no cloud queues, and zero document retention.

1. Summary of Data Practices

  • Zero Server Uploads: When you use any tool (Merge, Split, Compress, Sign, Convert), the binary file content is processed entirely in volatile client-side RAM. No file packets are transmitted over the internet to our servers.
  • No Account or Personal Information: We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or billing credentials to access any utility.
  • 0-Day Document Retention: Because no documents are ever transmitted to our servers, our server retention period is exactly zero seconds. Closing the browser tab destroys all in-memory file buffers immediately.
  • No Content Inspection: We do not scan, inspect, index, analyze, or run OCR algorithms on your documents for telemetry or profiling.

4. European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Disclosures

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Legal Basis for Processing: Because ZeroUpload does not collect or process your documents on remote servers, no document personal data is processed by our infrastructure. Standard web server logs (IP address, user-agent) are processed under the legal basis of Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to maintain site security, mitigate DDoS attacks, and deliver web assets reliably.
  • Data Subject Rights: You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any personal data held about you; the right to restrict or object to processing; and the right to data portability.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA) if you believe our data handling violates applicable privacy legislation.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Disclosures

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific statutory rights:

  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: ZeroUpload does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or document contents to any third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.
  • Right to Know & Delete: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal information is collected, and request deletion of that information. Because ZeroUpload operates anonymously without user accounts or document databases, we hold no document records linked to individual identities.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you in pricing, access, or quality of service for exercising your privacy rights.

6. Technical Infrastructure & Edge Network Logs

ZeroUpload is hosted via Cloudflare Pages and distributed edge delivery networks. Like virtually all web servers, the hosting infrastructure automatically records basic HTTP request headers when you visit the site. These logs may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamp. This data is used solely for traffic analytics, system health monitoring, and defense against malicious network attacks. These logs contain zero document data and are never correlated with your files.

7. Third-Party Open Source Libraries

ZeroUpload utilizes reputable open-source document libraries (such as pdf-lib, Mozilla pdf.js, mammoth.js, and WebAssembly binaries) loaded from secure content delivery networks. These client-side libraries run entirely within your local browser sandbox and do not transmit document content to any third-party endpoints.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical enhancements or regulatory changes. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
