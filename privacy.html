Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

ZeroUpload is built so that your files never leave your device. When you use a tool, everything happens inside your own browser. We do not upload, store, transmit, read, or track your documents — because there is no server waiting to receive them.

1. We never upload your files

Every tool is fully client-side. The code that merges, splits, compresses or converts your PDF runs locally on your computer using in-browser JavaScript libraries. Your files are processed in memory and are gone as soon as you close or refresh the page.

2. No document tracking

We do not collect, log, or track the content of your documents. We cannot read your files, and we do not store them. There are no analytics attached to your document data.

3. What we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information to use our tools. We have no access to them, and we have no way to link a document to a person.

4. Cookies & advertising

The site is free to use and is supported by advertising. Third-party ad providers may set cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. These cookies are controlled by the ad providers and are subject to their own privacy policies. We do not control or read these cookies.

5. Local usage data

Like most websites, our host may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and basic page-view logs to operate the service and keep it secure. This is standard hosting data and is not linked to your documents.

6. Third-party libraries

Our tools rely on well-known open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, docx and related) loaded from public CDNs. These libraries run on your device and do not receive your files.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through the contact page.
