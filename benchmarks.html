Reproducible browser fixture benchmarks

Measured October 19, 2026. These are single-run development checks, not typical-user speed claims or universal compression guarantees.

Environment and method

Windows, headless Chromium, local Wrangler Worker; single run after dependencies loaded. Chromium 156.0.8078.4.

Three Letter pages; each has a heading and 20 synthetic text lines; timestamps fixed. The image input is generated in a canvas. The Word and Unlock inputs come from earlier fixture conversions. Application libraries are served locally. Third-party advertising is stubbed to avoid creating test impressions.

Elapsed time begins at file selection, includes preview work and the scripted action, and ends when an output becomes available. It excludes initial page loading, download transfer, and human review. The script confirms nonempty outputs, expected PDF page counts, and Markdown marker content. These are modest smoke tests; they do not establish fidelity for every source layout, strength of encryption, legal validity, accessibility, or a complete sanitization guarantee.
