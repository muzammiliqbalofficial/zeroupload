About ZeroUpload

Free, private in-browser PDF utilities engineered with client-side WebAssembly.

ZeroUpload is a privacy-first suite of fifteen PDF tools: Sign PDF, Organize Pages, Page Numbers & Watermark, PDF to Markdown for AI, Merge, Split, Compress, PDF to Word, Word to PDF, PDF to Images, Images to PDF, Rotate, Remove Pages, Protect, and Unlock. They are 100% free, require no account registration, enforce no daily file quotas, and are built around one foundational principle: Your documents never leave your device.

The Architectural Principle: Everything Runs In Your Browser

Almost every online PDF website operates by uploading your sensitive documents to a remote cloud server, executing the transformation there, and transmitting a file back to you. That architecture exposes legal contracts, medical charts, tax records, and employee information to remote databases, unauthorized internal access, and potential cloud data breaches.

ZeroUpload takes the client-first approach instead. Every transformation runs exclusively in your browser using WebAssembly and modern JavaScript. There is zero upload step, zero network roundtrip, and zero document caching. Once a tool has finished loading, you can disconnect your Wi-Fi or mobile data completely and it keeps working, since nothing it does from that point on needs a network connection. Loading a different tool, or reloading the page, does need one again.

Open-Source Technical Foundations

ZeroUpload is built on established open-source document libraries:

  • pdf-lib: Low-level PDF object stream manipulation.
  • pdf.js: Mozilla's PDF rendering and text extraction engine.
  • mammoth & docx: Document parsing for Microsoft Word interoperability.
  • WebAssembly (Wasm): Compiled modules for client-side compression and processing.

Privacy By Design

Because ZeroUpload does not collect, transmit, or store your file content on any server, there's no upload for a breach, subpoena, or third party to reach — the document simply never leaves your device. That said, ZeroUpload is a tool, not a compliance program: whether your own use of it satisfies a specific framework like GDPR, CCPA, or HIPAA depends on your organization's broader practices, not on this site alone. If you operate under one of those frameworks, check with whoever handles your compliance before relying on any single tool.
