How ZeroUpload Works

Three simple steps to process PDF documents securely in your web browser.

STEP 1

Select Tool & Load Wasm

Choose from 15 PDF utilities. The tool loads high-speed JavaScript libraries directly into your browser engine. No software installation, plugins, or registration required.

STEP 2

Local Memory Processing

Drag & drop your documents. Files are read into an isolated local RAM ArrayBuffer. Document operations execute locally. Website hosting and advertising are separate network activities described in our privacy policy.

STEP 3

Instant Lossless Download

The compiled document is downloaded straight to your disk via a local synthetic blob. Closing the tab ends the editing session. Downloaded files remain on your device; secure memory erasure is not guaranteed.

Client-Side Security & Zero-Upload Guarantee

Local document processing reduces conversion-server exposure. Review browser, device, and delivery controls for confidential documents.

Zero Cloud Latency

No conversion-server upload is needed. Processing time depends on the document, selected operation, and available device memory. See our reproducible fixture benchmarks in the guides.

Local Editing Sessions

Selected documents are processed in browser memory. Closing the page ends the session, but downloaded files remain on disk and secure memory erasure is not guaranteed.

Local Processing After Loading

Operations can continue after required application libraries and workers have loaded. Test the needed tool before disconnecting; a fresh offline session is not guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding document security, memory performance, and legal compliance.

Is ZeroUpload really 100% private and how does it prove it?
Yes. ZeroUpload runs entirely inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript and browser APIs. Your documents are never sent over the internet or uploaded to any server. You can verify this by opening any tool, turning off your Wi-Fi or internet connection completely, and processing your document — it may continue to work after required dependencies have loaded. A complete offline installation is not guaranteed.
Are there any file size limits or daily quotas?
No. Unlike cloud PDF editors that charge subscription fees or limit you to 2 documents per day, ZeroUpload is completely free with no usage quotas. The only limit is your device's available random-access memory (RAM).
What happens to my documents after I close the browser tab?
Because files are held purely in volatile RAM, closing the browser tab or refreshing the page immediately destroys the in-memory buffer. ZeroUpload maintains zero document retention and zero server logs of your file content.
Are electronic signatures made on ZeroUpload legally binding?
Electronic signatures are recognized under the United States ESIGN Act and the European eIDAS regulation for general commercial agreements, NDAs, and forms. For high-stakes court filings or notarized deeds requiring formal identity verification certificates, specialized cryptographic signature services may be required.
Does ZeroUpload comply with GDPR and CCPA regulations?
Yes. Because ZeroUpload does not collect, process, or store personal document data on remote servers, it avoids the data exposure liabilities inherent in cloud platforms. Standard website hosting logs are kept strictly for network security and DDoS mitigation.
