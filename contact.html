Contact ZeroUpload

A bug, a question, or a tool you wish existed.

Email support@zeroupload.co. It reaches the person who builds and maintains the site, not a ticket queue. Expect a reply within two or three working days.

Please do not send us your files

ZeroUpload runs entirely in your browser, which means we never receive your documents and cannot open them to investigate. Sending one would hand us the very thing the site exists to avoid. Describe the problem instead.

Reporting a problem with a tool

Four details are usually enough to identify a bug without ever seeing the document:

  • Which tool, and what you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead, including any message on screen.
  • Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Windows, or Safari on an iPhone.
  • Roughly how big the file was and how many pages, since memory limits cause a lot of failures.

If you can reproduce it with a document that does not matter to you, such as a file you generated for the purpose, say so. That helps enormously.

Before you write

Two things solve most reports. First, reload the page and try once more: the tools load several libraries and an interrupted load leaves buttons that do nothing. Second, check the tool's own page, where the honest limits are written down. An empty Word conversion almost always means the PDF was a scan, and a compressed file that grew almost always means the original was plain text.

Other reasons to write

  • Suggest a tool. If something is missing, say what you need it for. That context decides what gets built next.
  • Report a security issue. Write with the details and give a reasonable window before disclosing publicly.
  • Privacy questions. The Privacy Policy covers what is and is not collected; ask if anything is unclear.
  • Legal or copyright matters. Use the same address and include enough detail to identify the issue.

The fastest way to get a useful answer: name the tool, describe what happened, and mention your browser. That is usually all it takes.
